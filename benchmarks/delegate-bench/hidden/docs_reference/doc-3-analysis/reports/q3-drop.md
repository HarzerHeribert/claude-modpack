# East: the largest revenue drop from Q2 to Q3

Counting completed rows only, with refunds as negative revenue, East fell the most of the five regions between the second
and the third quarter: from $333,747.57 to $234,325.08, a drop of $99,422.49 or 29.8 percent. The
product that accounts for most of it is Ledger Pro, whose revenue in East fell by $83,505.53, about 84 percent of the region's drop.
