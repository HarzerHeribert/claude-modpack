How the pack saves

Three ideas run through every mod: a step re-reads everything, what a step leaves behind is read again, and Claude follows orders but not advice.

Every step re-reads everything

A session is a loop. Claude gets the conversation so far, decides on one step, the tool answers, and the answer joins the conversation. Then the whole conversation goes back to the model for the next step. Caching makes the re-read cheap per token, not free, and it multiplies. So the unit of cost is the request: a mod that removes a step saves a whole re-read of everything.

Re-reading, priced

Worked out from Opus 5.5’s list prices: $0.20 per million tokens read from the cache, $8 to write them (one-hour lifetime). List prices. An illustration: what each step adds is your choice here, not a measurement.

Fewer steps, same work

Tokens per request barely moved with the pack; requests did. The same jobs, graded the same way, in fewer steps.

What a step leaves behind

A search’s output and the lines it read stay in the conversation, and every later request reads them again until a compaction removes them: the carry. In a long session it can cost more than the step itself. A mod that saves a step by adding text can lose money; seek’s first versions did, and seek as it ships leaves less behind.

Orders, not advice

Claude does not take advice from tools. It does take answers placed where it already reads (lsp-plus), and orders in your name that arrive with the prompt (delegate, route, seek).
