Claude Code, without the wasted steps.

See it work

A real Claude Code session with the add-ons on, cut together: a long job with a progress bar, edits and tests in one step, a plan built by a cheaper model, a text written to its word limit at once, a conversation tidied up in an instant, and the switchboard.

Why it costs less

Every time Claude takes a step (opens a file, makes an edit, runs the tests) it reads your whole conversation again. An hour into a session, that re-reading is most of what you pay for, whether you pay by plan or by token.

So the cheapest step is the one Claude never has to take. The add-ons take out the steps that need no thinking. The work comes out the same; Claude just gets there in fewer round trips.

Same job, same result. Fewer steps, so less to pay.

What you’ll notice

No new commands to learn. Things simply happen in fewer steps, and you can see more of what’s going on.

  • statuslineYou always know where you standOne line under the prompt shows how full the conversation is and how much of your plan’s five-hour and weekly use is left.
  • etaLong jobs tell you when they’ll finishA test run or a build in the background gets a progress bar and a finish time above the prompt.
  • cellsSeveral changes land as one stepEdits and the test run that checks them happen together, and every change still shows as a diff you can undo.
  • delegateA cheaper model builds what you approvedApprove a plan and a cheaper model does the building, while Claude checks the result before it reports back.
  • draftWord limits, met in one draftAsk for a text of at most so many words and it is written to fit the first time, not cut down in rounds of rewrites.
  • smart-compactNo waiting when a conversation gets longYour session is kept ready while you’re away, and when you turn it on, the summary of a long conversation is written in the background so tidying up is instant.

Changed your mind about one? /mods lists them all, and off means off, right away.

Questions people ask

Do I need to be a programmer?

You need Claude Code, in the terminal or the desktop app. Installing is one line you paste into it, and /modpack:setup walks you through the rest.

Does it cost anything?

The add-ons are free and open source. What Claude does still counts against your plan or your API bill, just less of it in a long session.

Will Claude’s work get worse?

Not that we have seen. In the batches that ran the same jobs both ways, 8 of 4078 of 4078 of 407

Does my work go anywhere new?

No. The add-ons run inside Claude Code on your computer. The one exception is route, which stays off unless you set it up: it asks an outside service, with your own key, whether a request is building work.

Can I switch them off?

Each one, any time: /mods shows the switches, and an add-on you turn off stops right away.
