The mods

Eight mods and the pack that installs them. Each one waits for a particular moment in a session, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

While you work, and while you’re away

  • statuslineWhile you work. One line under the prompt shows how full the conversation is and how much of your plan is used. Adds a view; nothing to measure.
  • etaWhen a long job runs. A bar and a finish time appear above the prompt. Adds a view; nothing to measure.
  • smart-compactWhen you step away. Your session is kept ready, or tidied up before it goes cold, whichever costs less. Keeping warm worked out from prices; compacting after a turn measured.

How they fit together

The mods never call each other. Each finds the others through Claude Code itself, and works alone when they are missing.

statusline is the line the others write on: smart-compact adds its ready or cold there. Edits made in a cell go through Claude Code’s Edit tool, so your permission prompts and /rewind apply. smart-compact keeps long sessions’ caches warm, where delegate and cells pay most. route gives delegate’s order for feature and test prompts, and seek’s setting rides with the same prompt.

What makes the cut

A mod joins the pack only when:

  • it was measured, and every graded run stayed correct;
  • it saves cost or time, makes the work more reliable, or only adds a view;
  • it works with the other mods and without them, and each part can be switched off;
  • it stays quiet where it has no proof that speaking helps.

Every option of every mod is in the README and SETUP.md.
