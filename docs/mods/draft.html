draft

Write to a word limit in one draft.

It comes with the pack and is on from the start.

The moment

You ask for a guide, a brief or a summary of at most 700 words.

Without draft

Claude writes all of it, counts, finds it too long, and writes all of it again: 1,052 → 798 → 757 → 694 words1,052 to 798 to 757 to 694 words1,052 to 798 to 757 to 694 words
1 task
Opus 5.5, 18 Oct 2026 in our test. Every rewrite costs the whole text in output, and every copy stays in the conversation for later steps to read again.

With draft

When your prompt sets a word limit, draft passes on your setting beside it: give each part its share of the words and aim a little under the limit, keep every fact the request asks for and cut wording, and if the facts cannot all fit, keep them and say how far over it runs. After each write of a Markdown or text file it shows the word count and, when the text is well over, each section’s target, so one more write lands it instead of cuts in rounds. It runs on your machine and sends nothing anywhere.

Turn it on or off

/mods draft off turns it off. order turns off the setting beside your prompt, gauge the word count after a write. Every option is in the README; the code is in draft/.
