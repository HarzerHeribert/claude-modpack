mods / modpack

modpack

Installs the others, switches them, keeps them current.

the packv0.6.010 suntil a hanging hook is skipped

The problem

Eight mods are eight things to install, update and switch, and another plugin’s hook that hangs can freeze Claude Code.

What it changes

One install brings all eight. /mods turns any of them on or off, right away. When another plugin’s permission or notification hook hangs, the pack stops waiting after 10 seconds and tells you which one it was. It checks for a new release once a day and updates itself, or only tells you.
