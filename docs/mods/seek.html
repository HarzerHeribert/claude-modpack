seek

Look around in few, lean steps.

It comes with the pack and is on from the start.

The moment

You ask for a change, and before touching anything Claude looks around: a search, a file, another search, another file.

Without seek

Each look is a step that re-reads the conversation. And whatever Claude reads stays in the conversation, read again by every later step until the session is compacted. A whole file opened for three lines is paid for again and again.

With seek

With your first prompt, and again as the session grows, seek passes on your setting: put the searches you can already name into one go, read only the lines that matter, and don’t read again what the session already holds. It runs on your machine and sends nothing anywhere.

Here is the same look around, without and with seek’s setting. Watch what stays behind.

Where it doesn’t help

The smallest lookups gain nothing, and sometimes take a step more. The first two versions of seek did the opposite of this one: they added the lines around a search’s hits, saved steps, and cost more, because the lines stayed behind. They are in the graveyard.

Turn it on or off

/mods seek off turns it off. map: true turns on the map of where a search’s hits sit, which saved nothing certain. Every option is in the README and SETUP.md; the code is in seek/.
